# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 27, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Fimbl app for iPhone and iPad and the website fimbl.app handle, why, who can see it, and how long we keep it. We have written it to be read, not skimmed past, and the AI features get their own section because they deserve one. If anything is unclear, [write to us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com).

## The short version

- **We collect what we need to run your diary:** your account, your profile and goals, what you log, how you feel, your weight and your story. If you start without an account, your diary stays on your phone until you make one (section 2.2).
- **Health and wellbeing data is sensitive.** We use it only to give you Fimbl, only with your consent, and never to sell it or to show you ads.
- **No ads and no ad trackers.** If you agree, the app sends usage statistics (which screens and features are used, never what you log) to PostHog in the EU, linked to your Fimbl account once you have one. Before you answer, it only counts which first-run steps are reached, anonymously (see section 2.1). You choose at the end of your first run and can change it any time in Settings. This website has no cookies or analytics.
- **AI is optional.** Everyone can try it: 3 free meal analyses and, with an account, a first weekly reflection. Premium adds the rest. These requests go from the app through our servers to Google's Gemini API. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, later free meal analyses go to Apple's model on Apple's Private Cloud Compute instead. Everything else in Fimbl works without any AI.
- **Apple Health data stays on your device,** with a few exceptions: the weight you log and weigh-ins imported from smart scales are saved to your account, and daily steps and active calories may be included in AI requests. Period days stay in Apple Health only.
- **Some things never leave your iPhone:** progress photos, meal and recipe photos, your cheerleaders and story photos, and period days.
- **You are in control.** Delete your account any time in Settings, and ask for a copy of your data any time by email.
- **When you delete your account,** your personal data is deleted. AI-generated content and AI usage data that we keep are anonymised, so they are no longer linked to you (see section 4.7).

### 2.1 Usage statistics

To learn how Fimbl is used and where it can get better, the app sends usage events to [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy), a product analytics service, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). There are two kinds.

**Anonymous first-run counts, before you answer.** While you go through your first run, the app counts which of its steps you reach, for example "logged a first meal" or "saw the daily targets", whether you started with or without an account, how you logged your first meal, how the subscription offer ended and how you answered the usage data question. Each count carries only the app version, the app language, how many seconds into the first run it was, and a random number that the app makes when it opens and forgets when it closes. That number is not stored on your device and is not linked to your device, your account or any other count, so the counts cannot tell who you are, and PostHog keeps no profile for them. If you answer that you don't want to share usage data, nothing more is counted.

**Usage statistics, only if you agree:**

- **A random ID for your device, linked to your account.** The app creates a random ID the first time it opens and keeps it on your device. When you have a Fimbl account, that ID is linked to your account's ID, so we can see how Fimbl is used over time; if you used Fimbl without an account first, those earlier events join your account too. Signing out stops the link for what follows. It is never linked to your email, your name or your RevenueCat ID. Deleting the app resets the device ID. If you agreed before October 25, 2026, when this question said the data was never linked to you, it stays unlinked unless you turn sharing off and on again in Settings.
- **What an event says:** that something happened, such as a screen opened, an onboarding step finished, a meal logged by barcode or by photo, a reflection opened or a purchase started, with simple labels and rounded counts (for example "meal: lunch", "2–3 items", "took 12 seconds"). It also includes the app version, device model, iOS version, app language, region setting, whether you use Premium and Calm or Classic mode, whether you use Fimbl without an account, whether a meal analysis was read by our servers or Apple's model, your plan type (lose, maintain or gain weight) and activity level, the general goals you picked in onboarding (such as more energy or a better routine) and how you tracked food before, and whether notifications and widgets are on.
- **Crash reports:** if the app crashes, a technical report of where in the code it happened.
- **What is never sent:** what you eat or search for, product names or barcodes, calories or amounts, your weight or body measurements, your feelings, hunger or mood, anything you write, AI conversations and reflections, photos, your name or email, and your precise location. We switch off IP-based location, and PostHog is set to discard your IP address.
- **Not used for tracking or ads.** We do not combine it with data from other apps or websites, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. PostHog processes it for us as our processor. The data is stored in the EU; PostHog Inc. is based in the United States, and any access from outside the EU is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing agreement.
- **How long:** for a limited period, currently up to 7 years, so we can compare how Fimbl changes over time. Deleting your account removes the link on your device; to have the usage data linked to your account deleted as well, write to us and we will delete it within a month. The anonymous first-run counts can't be linked to anyone, so they are kept.

**Your choice.** For the usage statistics, our legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). At the end of your first run, the "Help us make Fimbl even better" page asks you: *Continue* shares usage data, *Continue without sharing* doesn't. Until you answer, nothing but the anonymous first-run counts above is sent: the events from the first-run screens wait in the app's memory and are sent only if you agree, or thrown away if you don't. If you already used Fimbl before this page existed, nothing is sent unless you turn sharing on. You can change your answer at any time in Fimbl: Settings, Information, *Share usage data*. Turning it off stops all usage events and crash reports from that device straight away, and withdrawing consent does not affect what was sent before. The app keeps a note of your answer, when you gave it and which wording you saw on your device. For the anonymous first-run counts, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in seeing where new users stop (Art. 6(1)(f)); they cannot identify you, so there is nothing of yours in them to access or delete.

### 2.2 Starting without an account

You can log your first meals before you make an account. Until you do:

- **Your diary stays on your phone.** Meals, check-ins, your plan, moods and favorites are kept in the app's storage on your iPhone or iPad and included in your device backup, because that is the only copy. Some features, such as weight, progress photos, Your story, body notes and reflections, need an account.
- **What reaches our servers:** food searches and barcode lookups (which read the shared food database), and the meal analyses you choose to run, under a guest ID (section 4).
- **The guest ID** is a random number the app creates and keeps in your device's Keychain, so it stays even if you delete and reinstall the app and the free analyses are not reset. Our server keeps a guest record under it, with no name, email or password, the IP address and user agent of each session, and how many free analyses you have used. It is never linked to your Apple ID.
- **When you make an account,** the app uploads your diary to it, your free analyses and their usage log move to the account, and the guest record is deleted.
- **If you never make one,** deleting the app removes the diary from the device (backups excepted). Starting over in the app clears the phone but not the guest record. We delete guest records that have not been used for 180 days.

### Health and wellbeing data

Your weight, hunger, feelings, mood, symptoms and cycle can reveal things about your health, so the GDPR treats them as special category data. We process them only with your explicit consent, which you give when you choose to enter them and grant permissions. Without some of this data, such as your height and weight, Fimbl cannot calculate your targets. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting entries, switching off a feature (for example mood check-ins, period tracking or Health sync) or deleting your account. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before. We never sell this data and never use it for advertising.

### Products you add

If you add a missing product (name, brand, barcode and nutrition), it is stored in the Fimbl food database, which is shared by all Fimbl users, so another person who scans the same barcode may see it. Please do not put personal information in product names.

### What stays on your device

Some things never leave your iPhone or iPad:

- **Progress photos.** They are never uploaded to our servers, never used for AI and never included in iCloud backups.
- **Meal photos** from scans and the photos you add to recipes.
- **Your cheerleaders** (their names and photos) and the photos in your story.
- **Period days**, kept in Apple Health (section 3).
- Your sign-in tokens and guest ID (in the iOS Keychain), your healthier-swap history and saved swaps, and your diary while you use Fimbl without an account.
- The snapshot that powers your widgets, which can show today's meals and calories, your weight trend, steps and streak, your why, your latest story line and your cheerleaders. Widgets can appear on your Lock Screen, so choose them with that in mind.

They are removed when you delete the app or your account, except the guest ID (see above) and anything you saved elsewhere yourself.

## 3 Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and you choose what Fimbl can access in iOS.

- **What Fimbl reads:** steps, walking and running distance, active energy, resting energy, workouts with their distance and, on a workout's page, its route, heart rate and other workout measurements, your body weight (to import weigh-ins from smart scales and other apps), and menstrual flow if you track your period.
- **What Fimbl writes,** when you turn on Health sync in Settings: the weight you log, and for each diary entry its energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat with the food's name. Sharing meals and importing weigh-ins are on by default with Health sync, and you can turn each off. If you track your period in body notes, the days you log are written to Apple Health.
- **How we use it:** to show your activity, statistics, cycle and widgets. The raw Health samples, workout routes and heart rate are not uploaded to our servers.
- **Imported weigh-ins** are the exception: a weigh-in from a smart scale or another app is saved to your account like one you logged, with the name of the app that recorded it, so it appears in your progress, reflections and export.
- **Period days stay in Apple Health.** They are never sent to our servers, to AI or to usage statistics, and are not part of the data export.
- **Exception for AI:** if Health sync is on and you use an AI feature that personalises its answer (reflections, the coach, recipe chat, recipe import and Calm mode grades), daily step counts and active calories for the last 14 days can be included in the request. Reflections keep a few numbers derived from them, such as your average steps.
- **What we never do:** sell Health data, use it for advertising or marketing, or share it with data brokers or advertising platforms.

You can stop this at any time in Fimbl (Settings, Health sync) or in iOS (Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, Fimbl). What Fimbl wrote to Apple Health stays there if you stop syncing or delete your account; you can delete it in the Health app.

## 4 How AI works in Fimbl

Some features use AI. This section explains how we built them, what each one sees, where the data goes and what we keep. The short version: **AI is optional, everyone can try it a few times for free, most of it is part of Premium, and it never sees your name or email.**

### 4.1 How we built it

We did not build our own AI model, and we do not train one on your data. Fimbl's features use a hosted model, Google's Gemini, through its developer API. When you use a feature, the app sends your request to our servers. Our servers add our instructions (written to be warm, non-judgemental, free of diet-culture language and free of medical claims), include only the context that feature needs, call Gemini, and turn the answer into a structured result such as a calorie estimate, a recipe or a reflection, which comes back to your app. The app itself never holds any AI provider key. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, some free meal analyses use Apple's model instead (section 4.2).

### 4.2 When AI is used

- **Free tries for everyone.** Every account, and anyone using Fimbl without one, gets 3 free meal analyses (a photo scan or a described meal), and every account gets one free weekly reflection, made when you open it after your first week. They work like the Premium features below, on a lighter Gemini model. Our servers count them under your user ID, or your guest ID if you have no account (section 2.2).
- **Apple Intelligence for later free analyses.** On an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with iOS 27 and Apple Intelligence turned on, free meal analyses after the first three are read by Apple's model on Apple's Private Cloud Compute. The app sends only the photo or your words and the app's language, no profile or goals, straight to Apple; the request doesn't pass through our servers. Apple [describes Private Cloud Compute](https://security.apple.com/blog/private-cloud-compute/) as using the data only to answer the request, without keeping it or making it available to Apple. If Apple's service is unavailable or its daily limit is reached, the app sends the request to our servers instead, where it is refused if you have no free analyses left.
- **Everything else is Premium.** Premium access, including a free trial, is checked on our servers for every other AI request.
- **On request:** photo and nutrition-label scans, describing a meal, recipe import, recipe chat, coach chat and healthier swaps run only when you use them.
- **Automatically for Premium accounts:** your weekly reflection is generated after each week ends (or when you tap Reflect), and Calm mode grades are generated as you log, if you have chosen Calm mode.
- **Not an automated decision-maker.** Fimbl does not use AI to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Grades, reflections and estimates are suggestions you can ignore or change.

There is no single AI switch in the app today. If you would rather not have any AI processing, don't use photo scans or meal descriptions, don't open the free reflection, don't subscribe to Premium and stay in Classic mode, or [tell us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) and we will work with you to turn it off.

### 4.4 Who processes it

Google acts as our processor for the Gemini API and the Custom Search API. We use these services under Google's terms for developers, which describe how Google handles the data it receives. See [Google's Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). We do not use your data to train or fine-tune any AI model, and we do not allow anyone else to use it for their own purposes.

Free meal analyses on Apple's model are processed by Apple on Private Cloud Compute under [Apple's privacy terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) (section 4.2). We receive nothing from those requests.

### 4.5 Limits and logs

To keep Fimbl fair and affordable we apply fair-use limits per person and per feature. For that we keep a usage log: who used which feature (your user ID, or your guest ID), when, which model, whether it was a free try, how large the request was, the app's user agent, and how many tokens and what cost it used. A separate record counts the free tries you have used. **The log does not contain your photos, text, transcripts or answers.** We keep it for a limited period, currently up to 400 days (about 13 months), and then delete it. If you delete your account, the link between the log and you is removed (see section 4.7).

### 4.6 Accuracy and safety

AI makes mistakes. Calorie and nutrition estimates can be a little off, recipes can contain errors, and reflections can misread a week, so please adjust anything that doesn't look right. Fimbl's AI offers general guidance and is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and the coach is not a therapist. If you are struggling with food, your body or your mood, please talk to a healthcare professional. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

### 4.7 AI data after you delete your account

We store AI-generated content and AI usage data with your account, and we keep it so we can run, check and improve Fimbl's AI features. This covers things like reflections, coach and recipe conversations, Calm mode grades, recipe import results and usage logs. It helps us measure quality and safety, fix problems in our instructions, understand costs and prevent abuse.

- **While your account exists,** this data belongs to your account, and you can delete a conversation in the app at any time.
- **When you delete your account,** we delete your personal data: your account, profile, diary, wellbeing entries, weight and goals. The AI data described above is not deleted. Instead we anonymise it: we remove the link to your account and the identifiers we hold, such as your user ID, so it is no longer attributed to you. We do not keep your name, email address or profile together with it.
- **Anonymised data** is no longer personal data. We may keep it for as long as it is useful, and we use it to evaluate and improve our features and safeguards. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to train or fine-tune AI models.
- **Your choice.** Conversations are free text, so please avoid writing names or other identifying details in them. If you would rather have this data deleted instead of anonymised, or you object to us keeping it, [email us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) and we will handle your request as the law requires.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving a safe, reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f)), and, for health-related content, the explicit consent you give when you choose to use AI features (Art. 9(2)(a)). Anonymising the data is how we protect your privacy while doing so.

### 4.8 Fimbl without AI

Everything outside the AI features works without any data going to an AI provider: your diary, food search, barcode scanning, adding products, calorie quick adds, saved meals, favorites, manual recipes, weight, mood and meal check-ins, body notes, the Calm view's balance marks, Your story, progress photos, activity, goals and streaks, statistics, the First Week journey, widgets and Shortcuts. If your Premium ends, Premium AI features stop and nothing more is sent.

## 5 Permissions and notifications

- **Camera:** only when you scan a barcode or photograph a meal or product.
- **Photos:** the pictures you choose to share with Fimbl (for a scan, a recipe, your story or a cheerleader). If you allow full access, Fimbl also creates and reads its own albums for the progress photos you save there, and share cards you save are added to your library.

- **Notifications:** Fimbl's reminders (streak milestones, First Week nudges at the time you choose, your weekly story card, a letter to your future self on the day it opens, the monthly look-back, a gentle note if you have been away, a reflection that is ready, and a reminder before a free trial ends) are scheduled on your device. - **Sharing:** when you share a card to Instagram, Facebook, TikTok or another app, iOS hands the image to that app (for Instagram and Facebook Stories, together with Fimbl's Meta app ID). What happens next is covered by that app's privacy policy. Nothing is uploaded to us.

You can change any permission in iOS Settings at any time.

## 6 Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a few service providers who process data on our behalf:

- **Google:** Sign in with Google, the Gemini API and Custom Search for AI features (section 4), and a favicon service that loads shop icons in healthier swaps.
- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and subscriptions, speech recognition for voice input, and Private Cloud Compute for free meal analyses on iPhones with Apple Intelligence (section 4.2).
- **PostHog:** usage statistics and crash reports if you agree, linked to your account once you have one, and anonymous first-run counts, hosted in the EU (section 2.1).
- **RevenueCat:** subscription management. It receives your Fimbl user ID and your purchase information, and nothing else we hold about you.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers:** the servers and database behind api.fimbl.app, operated by us with providers acting under our instructions.
- **Cloudflare:** sits in front of api.fimbl.app to protect it, and hosts the promo film on fimbl.app (see section 13).

Some screens show pictures loaded directly from other websites, such as product images in healthier swaps or recipe images. Those websites can see your IP address when your phone loads the image. We may also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect our rights, to the extent the law allows.

## 7 Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some of our providers, including Google, Apple, RevenueCat and Cloudflare, may process data outside the European Union and European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where that happens we rely on an adequacy decision (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, together with additional safeguards where needed.

## 9 Deleting and exporting your data

- **Delete your account:** open Settings in Fimbl and choose Delete account. This deletes your account and your personal data, and on your phone your progress photos, meal and recipe photos, cheerleaders and story photos. AI data we keep is anonymised, so it is no longer linked to you (section 4.7). Usage statistics you agreed to share stay in PostHog until you ask us to delete them (section 2.1). If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Fimbl's access to your Apple account.
- **Your subscription is separate.** Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel in the App Store (Settings, your name, Subscriptions).
- **What stays elsewhere:** what Fimbl wrote to Apple Health, photos and cards you saved to your photo library, and files you exported stay where they are. Delete them there if you want.
- **Deleting the app** removes the data stored on your device (including a diary kept without an account), but not your account. Delete the account first if you want your personal data deleted.
- **Export it yourself:** in Fimbl's Settings you can export your diary, check-ins, moods, symptoms, weigh-ins and goals as CSV files, and back up your progress, meal and recipe photos to a file. Both are made on your phone and saved where you choose. The photo backup file is not encrypted, so keep it somewhere safe.
- **Get a full copy:** for everything else, such as your profile, your story or your reflections, [email us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) any time and we will send you your data in a common, machine-readable format, within the time the law requires (normally one month).

## 10 Security

Everything that travels between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain, the app uses iOS file protection, passwords are stored hashed, and access to our servers is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If you notice a problem, please tell us, and if a breach affects you we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

- **Erase** your data (Art. 17), for example by deleting your account.

- **Receive** your data in a portable format (Art. 20).
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

- **Complain** to a supervisory authority (Art. 77). In Finland that is the [Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/). You can also complain to the authority in your own country.
