# Privacy Policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Fimbl app for iPhone and iPad and the website fimbl.app handle, why, who can see it, and how long we keep it. We have written it to be read, not skimmed past, and the AI features get their own section because they deserve one. If anything is unclear, [write to us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com).

## The short version

- **We collect what we need to run your diary:** your account, your profile and goals, what you log, how you feel, and your weight.
- **Health and wellbeing data is sensitive.** We use it only to give you Fimbl, only with your consent, and never to sell it or to show you ads.
- **No ads and no ad trackers.** If you agree, the app sends anonymous usage statistics (which screens and features are used, never what you log) to PostHog in the EU. They are not linked to your account and cannot be used to identify you (see section 2.1). You choose during setup and can change it any time in Settings. This website has no cookies or analytics.
- **AI features are part of Premium and entirely optional.** When one is used, the data it needs goes from the app through our servers to Google's Gemini API. Everything else in Fimbl works without any AI.
- **Apple Health data stays on your device,** with two exceptions: the weight you log, and daily steps and active calories that may be included in AI requests if you turned Health sync on and use a Premium AI feature.
- **You are in control.** Delete your account any time in Settings, and ask for a copy of your data any time by email.
- **When you delete your account,** your personal data is deleted. AI-generated content and AI usage data that we keep are anonymised, so they are no longer linked to you (see section 4.7).

### 2.1 Anonymous usage statistics

To learn how Fimbl is used and where it can get better, and only if you agree, the app sends anonymous usage events to [PostHog](https://posthog.com/privacy), a product analytics service, hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt). We built it so the data cannot be tied to you:

- **A random ID, not your account.** The app creates a random ID the first time it opens and keeps it only on your device. It is never linked to your Fimbl account, your email, your name or your RevenueCat ID. Deleting the app or your account resets it, after which you are counted as someone new.
- **What an event says:** that something happened, such as a screen opened, an onboarding step finished, a meal logged by barcode or by photo, a reflection opened or a purchase started, with simple labels and rounded counts (for example "meal: lunch", "2–3 items", "took 12 seconds"). It also includes the app version, device model, iOS version, app language, region setting, whether you use Premium and Calm or Classic mode, your plan type (lose, maintain or gain weight) and activity level, the general goals you picked in onboarding (such as more energy or a better routine) and how you tracked food before, and whether notifications and widgets are on.
- **Crash reports:** if the app crashes, a technical report of where in the code it happened.
- **Screen recordings in test versions only:** in TestFlight beta versions of Fimbl, and only if you agree to share usage data, PostHog also records how the screens look and where you tap, so we can find confusing spots and bugs. All text, input fields, images and camera views are covered on your device before anything is sent, so a recording shows the layout, not what you eat, write or photograph. The version from the App Store does not record screens. Recordings are deleted after 30 days.
- **What is never sent:** what you eat or search for, product names or barcodes, calories or amounts, your weight or body measurements, your feelings, hunger or mood, anything you write, AI conversations and reflections, photos, your name, email or account ID, and your precise location. We switch off IP-based location, and PostHog is set to discard your IP address.
- **Not used for tracking or ads.** We do not combine it with data from other apps or websites, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. PostHog processes it for us as our processor. The data is stored in the EU; PostHog Inc. is based in the United States, and any access from outside the EU is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in its data processing agreement.
- **How long:** for a limited period, currently up to 7 years, so we can compare how Fimbl changes over time. Because it is not linked to you, deleting your account does not delete it.

**Your choice.** Our legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). During setup, the "Help us make Fimbl even better" step asks you: *Continue* shares anonymous usage data, *Continue without sharing* doesn't. Until you answer, nothing is stored or sent: the few events from the setup screens wait in the app's memory and are sent only if you agree, or thrown away if you don't. If you already used Fimbl before this step existed, nothing is sent unless you turn sharing on. You can change your answer at any time in Fimbl: Settings, Information, *Share anonymous usage data*. Turning it off stops all usage events and crash reports from that device straight away, and withdrawing consent does not affect what was sent before. The app keeps a note of your answer and when you gave it on your device. Because the data is designed not to identify you, we usually cannot find "your" events if you ask us about them.

### Health and wellbeing data

Your weight, hunger, feelings and mood can reveal things about your health, so the GDPR treats them as special category data. We process them only with your explicit consent, which you give when you choose to enter them and grant permissions. Without some of this data, such as your height and weight, Fimbl cannot calculate your targets. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting entries, switching off a feature (for example mood check-ins or Health sync) or deleting your account. Withdrawing does not affect what we did before. We never sell this data and never use it for advertising.

### Products you add

If you add a missing product (name, brand, barcode and nutrition), it is stored in the Fimbl food database, which is shared by all Fimbl users, so another person who scans the same barcode may see it. Please do not put personal information in product names.

### What stays on your device

Some things never leave your iPhone or iPad: your sign-in tokens (kept in the iOS Keychain), thumbnails of meals you scanned, your healthier-swap history, saved foods and swap preferences, your First Week journey progress, and the small snapshot that powers your widgets. They are removed when you delete the app.

## 3 Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional, and you choose what Fimbl can access in iOS.

- **What Fimbl reads:** steps, walking and running distance, active energy, resting energy and workouts (including cycling distance).
- **What Fimbl writes:** your weight only, and only when you turn on Health sync in Settings. Fimbl does not write food or nutrition to Apple Health.
- **How we use it:** to show your activity, statistics and widgets. The raw Health samples are not uploaded to our servers.
- **Exception for AI:** if Health sync is on and you use a Premium AI feature that personalises its answer (reflections, the coach, recipe chat, recipe import and Calm mode grades), daily step counts and active calories for the last 14 days can be included in the request. Reflections keep a few numbers derived from them, such as your average steps.
- **What we never do:** sell Health data, use it for advertising or marketing, or share it with data brokers or advertising platforms.

You can stop this at any time in Fimbl (Settings, Health sync) or in iOS (Settings, Health, Data Access & Devices, Fimbl).

## 4 How AI works in Fimbl

Some Premium features use AI. This section explains how we built them, what each one sees, where the data goes and what we keep. The short version: **AI is optional, only used by Premium accounts, and never sees your name or email.**

### 4.1 How we built it

We did not build our own AI model, and we do not train one on your data. Fimbl's features use a hosted model, Google's Gemini, through its developer API. When you use a feature, the app sends your request to our servers. Our servers add our instructions (written to be warm, non-judgemental, free of diet-culture language and free of medical claims), include only the context that feature needs, call Gemini, and turn the answer into a structured result such as a calorie estimate, a recipe or a reflection, which comes back to your app. The app itself never holds any AI provider key.

### 4.2 When AI is used

- **Premium only.** Premium access is checked on our servers for every AI request. Free accounts never send anything to an AI service.
- **On request:** photo and nutrition-label scans, describing a meal, recipe import, recipe chat, coach chat and healthier swaps run only when you use them.
- **Automatically for Premium accounts:** your weekly reflection is generated after each week ends (or when you tap Reflect), and Calm mode grades are generated as you log, if you have chosen Calm mode.
- **Not an automated decision-maker.** Fimbl does not use AI to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. Grades, reflections and estimates are suggestions you can ignore or change.

There is no single AI switch in the app today. If you would rather not have any AI processing, don't subscribe to Premium, stay in Classic mode, or [tell us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) and we will work with you to turn it off.

### 4.3 What each feature sees and what we keep

| Feature | What is sent to the AI | What we store |
|---|---|---|
| Photo calorie scan and nutrition-label scan | The photo, resized and stripped of location and camera metadata, and your language. The model may use Google Search to check nutrition facts. | The photo is used to produce the result and is not saved to your account. If you save the estimate, the numbers are saved as a quick-add entry, and a small thumbnail stays on your phone. |
| Describe a meal, or say it | The text, up to 500 characters. Voice is turned into text by Apple's speech recognition on your iPhone (Apple may process audio on its servers, depending on your device and language, under Apple's privacy terms). The audio itself never goes to us or to Gemini. | Nothing from the request. |
| Weekly reflections | Your diary for the week (foods, times, amounts, nutrition), meal check-ins, mood scores and notes, weigh-ins and goal weight, goals, profile details such as age, gender, activity level, plan and onboarding answers, and Health steps and active calories if Health sync is on. | The reflection text and a few summary numbers, in your account. |
| Reflection coach chat | Your message (up to 1,200 characters), the last 14 days of the context above, up to four recent reflections and the earlier messages in that conversation. | The conversation: your messages, replies, short reasoning summaries and usage figures. You can delete a conversation in the app. |
| Recipe chat | Your message, profile, goals, 7-day averages and top foods, and the recipe you are working on. | The conversation, as above. |
| Recipe import from a link or photo | The link or photo you share. Our server fetches the page or reads the caption itself. For a video it may download the video temporarily and pass it to Gemini. | The import and its result in your account. Temporary video copies are removed automatically after processing. The recipe result is cached by link, language and units for a limited time (currently up to 180 days) so the same link is not analysed twice. The cache holds the recipe, not your diary or profile. |

Our usage logs record the internal user ID. |
| Calm mode grades | That day's meals and check-ins, your profile and goals, weight trend, recent moods and the previous six days. | The grade, a short explanation, the nutrition and check-in it was based on, and usage figures, in your account. |

**What is never sent to the AI:** your name, your email address, your height or your date of birth (we send an age instead).

What happens to the AI data we store when you delete your account is explained in section 4.7.

### 4.4 Who processes it

Google acts as our processor for the Gemini API and the Custom Search API. We use these services under Google's terms for developers, which describe how Google handles the data it receives. See [Google's Gemini API terms](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/terms). We do not use your data to train or fine-tune any AI model, and we do not allow anyone else to use it for their own purposes.

### 4.5 Limits and logs

To keep Fimbl fair and affordable we apply fair-use limits per person and per feature. For that we keep a usage log: who used which feature, when, which model, how large the request was, and how many tokens and what cost it used. **The log does not contain your photos, text, transcripts or answers.** We keep it for a limited period, currently up to 400 days (about 13 months), and then delete it. If you delete your account, the link between the log and you is removed (see section 4.7).

### 4.6 Accuracy and safety

AI makes mistakes. Calorie and nutrition estimates can be a little off, recipes can contain errors, and reflections can misread a week, so please adjust anything that doesn't look right. Fimbl's AI offers general guidance and is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, and the coach is not a therapist. If you are struggling with food, your body or your mood, please talk to a healthcare professional. If you are in crisis, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line.

### 4.7 AI data after you delete your account

We store AI-generated content and AI usage data with your account, and we keep it so we can run, check and improve Fimbl's AI features. This covers things like reflections, coach and recipe conversations, Calm mode grades, recipe import results and usage logs. It helps us measure quality and safety, fix problems in our instructions, understand costs and prevent abuse.

- **While your account exists,** this data belongs to your account, and you can delete a conversation in the app at any time.
- **When you delete your account,** we delete your personal data: your account, profile, diary, wellbeing entries, weight and goals. The AI data described above is not deleted. Instead we anonymise it: we remove the link to your account and the identifiers we hold, such as your user ID, so it is no longer attributed to you. We do not keep your name, email address or profile together with it.
- **Anonymised data** is no longer personal data. We may keep it for as long as it is useful, and we use it to evaluate and improve our features and safeguards. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to train or fine-tune AI models.
- **Your choice.** Conversations are free text, so please avoid writing names or other identifying details in them. If you would rather have this data deleted instead of anonymised, or you object to us keeping it, [email us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) and we will handle your request as the law requires.

Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running and improving a safe, reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f)), and, for health-related content, the explicit consent you give when you choose to use AI features (Art. 9(2)(a)). Anonymising the data is how we protect your privacy while doing so.

### 4.8 Fimbl without AI

Everything outside the Premium AI features works without any data going to an AI provider: your diary, food search, barcode scanning, adding products, calorie quick adds, saved meals, manual recipes, weight, mood and meal check-ins, activity, goals and streaks, statistics, the First Week journey and Siri shortcuts for quick adds and weigh-ins. If your Premium ends, AI features stop and nothing more is sent.

## 6 Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a few service providers who process data on our behalf:

- **Google:** Sign in with Google, the Gemini API and Custom Search for AI features (section 4), and a favicon service that loads shop icons in healthier swaps.
- **Apple:** Sign in with Apple, App Store payments and subscriptions, and speech recognition for voice input.
- **PostHog:** anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, hosted in the EU (section 2.1).
- **RevenueCat:** subscription management. It receives your Fimbl user ID and your purchase information, and nothing else we hold about you.
- **Hosting and infrastructure providers:** the servers and database behind api.fimbl.app, operated by us with providers acting under our instructions.
- **Cloudflare:** hosts the promo film on fimbl.app (see section 13).

Some screens show pictures loaded directly from other websites, such as product images in healthier swaps or recipe images. Those websites can see your IP address when your phone loads the image. We may also disclose data where the law requires it or to protect our rights, to the extent the law allows.

## 9 Deleting and exporting your data

- **Delete your account:** open Settings in Fimbl and choose Delete account. This deletes your account and your personal data. AI data we keep is anonymised, so it is no longer linked to you (section 4.7). If you signed in with Apple, we also revoke Fimbl's access to your Apple account.
- **Your subscription is separate.** Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel in the App Store (Settings, your name, Subscriptions).
- **Deleting the app** removes the data stored on your device, but not your account. Delete the account first if you want your personal data deleted.
- **Get a copy:** there is no export button yet, but you can [email us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) any time and we will send you your data in a common, machine-readable format, within the time the law requires (normally one month).

## 10 Security

Everything that travels between the app and our servers is encrypted with TLS. Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS Keychain, the app uses iOS file protection, passwords are stored hashed, and access to our servers is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly secure. If you notice a problem, please tell us, and if a breach affects you we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 11 Age and children

Fimbl is for people aged 13 and over, which is the age of digital consent in Finland. We do not knowingly collect personal data from younger children. If you believe a child has given us data, [contact us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com) and we will delete it. Fimbl is not designed for treating eating disorders, so please talk to a professional if that is a concern for you or someone you care about.

## 12 Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **Access** the data we hold about you and get a copy (Art. 15).
- **Correct** inaccurate data (Art. 16). Most of it you can edit directly in the app.
- **Erase** your data (Art. 17), for example by deleting your account.
- **Restrict** how we process it (Art. 18).
- **Receive** your data in a portable format (Art. 20).
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).
- **Withdraw consent** at any time, with effect for the future (Art. 7(3)).
- **Complain** to a supervisory authority (Art. 77). In Finland that is the [Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman](https://tietosuoja.fi/en/). You can also complain to the authority in your own country.

Anonymised data is no longer personal data, so these rights do not apply to it. See section 4.7 for your options.

To use any of these rights, [email us](mailto:appriseven@gmail.com). We may need to confirm it is really you, and we will reply within one month.

## 13 This website

The website fimbl.app sets no cookies and has no analytics, advertising trackers or social media widgets. Its fonts are hosted on the site itself. If you pick a language, your browser remembers that choice in its local storage on your device so the site can open in that language next time; it is never sent to us, and you can clear it with your browser data. The promo film is the one thing loaded from elsewhere: it comes from video.fimbl.app, hosted by Cloudflare, and only when you press play. Then Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser details to deliver the video. The site is served by GitHub Pages, and GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when you visit, as described in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We do not see or keep that data.
