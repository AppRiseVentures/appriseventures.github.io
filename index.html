FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Fimbl free?

Yes. Fimbl is free to download, and you can log meals, moods, weight and activity at no cost. Fimbl Premium adds extras like macro goals, photo calorie scan, voice logging, weekly AI reflections, widgets and streak restore, and starts with a 7-day free trial.

Is Fimbl a diet app?

Not in the usual sense. You can set a calorie goal if it helps you, but Fimbl is about balance, awareness and habits. It's built to support a healthier relationship with food, never to make you feel bad about it.

Which devices does Fimbl work on?

Fimbl is designed natively for iPhone and iPad, in light and dark mode, with Home and Lock Screen widgets.

Does Fimbl work with Apple Health?

Yes. With your permission, Fimbl reads your steps and active and resting energy, and keeps your weight in sync, so your activity and nutrition live in one place.

What happens to my data?

Your data is encrypted in transit, and your health data is never shared with third parties. You can delete your account and all your data at any time from the app's settings. Read the full privacy policy.
